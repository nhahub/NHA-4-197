About Us

About Green Cycle

Green Cycle is a newly launched, student-led digital initiative created by passionate participants of the DEPI Scholarship. Our vision is to empower individuals to make sustainable daily choices through accessible, cutting-edge technology. By leveraging lightweight AI models, we aim to guide users in waste sorting, pollution severity evaluation, and environmental simulations, establishing a strong foundation for green and smart communities of tomorrow.
